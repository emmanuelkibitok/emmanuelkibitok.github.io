About me

I am Emmanuel Kibitok, a Software Developer based in Kenya with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology.

My background spans software development, ICT support, database systems, and technology consulting. I work with PHP and MySQL and continue to strengthen my backend development skills through practical projects.

As the founder of Havana Technologies Company Limited, I help clients understand their technology needs and coordinate the delivery of software and database solutions.
